Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

The short version. We keep what you give us and what the app needs to work: your email, your profile, your fishing log, and the spots you mark. Your spots and catches are private by default — they go to other anglers only when you choose to share them. We don't sell anything to advertisers. Catch photos are stored so they stay with your catch, and are shared exactly as that catch is shared.

Who we are

ElavoFishAI is operated by Elavo AI. If you have a question about this policy or your data, contact us at the address on the app's sign-in page.

Catch photos

When you photograph a catch, the image is sent to our AI provider so it can identify the species and estimate the size, and — when you save the catch — stored with it, so the photo stays with the record and appears alongside it for anyone you shared that catch with.

Two things we do deliberately. The photo is resized in your browser before it leaves your device, which also removes the EXIF data — including the GPS coordinates most phones write into a photo — so a shared picture cannot give away a spot your sharing settings are protecting. And photos are served through the app rather than by a public link, so a photo shared with friends stays that way; a public link, once created, cannot be taken back.

Delete a photo and it is removed from our storage. Photos uploaded but never attached to a catch are deleted automatically within a day.

Location

The app asks for your device location only when you tap something that needs it — “Find me”, “Use my location”, dropping a launch pin. We don't track you in the background, and we never collect location while the app is closed.

Who can see your fishing data

Every spot, catch and waypoint carries its own visibility, and you set the default:

  • Just me — nobody else, ever.
  • Friends — anglers whose friend request you accepted.
  • A group — one crew you created.
  • Everyone — any signed-in angler.

You control separately whether you appear in search (anyone, friends of friends, or nobody), and who can message you. Blocking someone hides you from each other completely. Changing a default doesn't change what you already shared — those keep the visibility they had.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and log — until you delete them.
  • Sign-in links — 15 minutes, then swept.
  • Fetched agency reports — 120 days. Reports you posted are kept.
  • Error reports — 14 days.
  • Backups — up to 14 days, after which they are deleted.

Your choices

  • See and change everything about you in Settings → Profile.
  • Export your log and waypoints from Settings → Data & export, as JSON, CSV or GPX.
  • Delete your account by contacting us; your trips, spots, waypoints, messages and reports go with it. Messages you sent may remain in the other angler's copy of the conversation.
  • Stop email by not requesting sign-in links — we send no marketing email.

Security, honestly stated

Sign-in uses one-time links rather than passwords, sessions are stored as hashes, admin access needs a password plus an emailed code, and secrets in our configuration are encrypted at rest. Traffic is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we won't claim otherwise; if we ever discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you.

Children

ElavoFishAI is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app rather than quietly editing this page.
