Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

The short version. We keep what you give us and what the app needs to work: your email, your profile, your fishing log, and the spots you mark. Your spots and catches are private by default — they go to other anglers only when you choose to share them. We don't sell anything to advertisers. Catch photos are stored so they stay with your catch, and are shared exactly as that catch is shared.

Who we are

ElavoFishAI is operated by Elavo AI. If you have a question about this policy or your data, contact us at the address on the app's sign-in page.

Catch photos

When you photograph a catch, the image is sent to our AI provider so it can identify the species and estimate the size, and — when you save the catch — stored with it, so the photo stays with the record and appears alongside it for anyone you shared that catch with.

Two things we do deliberately. The photo is resized in your browser before it leaves your device, which also removes the EXIF data — including the GPS coordinates most phones write into a photo — so a shared picture cannot give away a spot your sharing settings are protecting. And photos are served through the app rather than by a public link, so a photo shared with friends stays that way; a public link, once created, cannot be taken back.

Delete a photo and it is removed from our storage. Photos uploaded but never attached to a catch are deleted automatically within a day.

Location

The app asks for your device location only when you tap something that needs it — “Find me”, “Use my location”, dropping a launch pin. We don't track you in the background, and we never collect location while the app is closed.

Who can see your fishing data

Every spot, catch and waypoint carries its own visibility, and you set the default:

  • Just me — nobody else, ever.
  • Friends — anglers whose friend request you accepted.
  • A group — one crew you created.
  • Everyone — any signed-in angler.

You control separately whether you appear in search (anyone, friends of friends, or nobody), and who can message you. Blocking someone hides you from each other completely. Changing a default doesn't change what you already shared — those keep the visibility they had.

Posts, pages and comments

A post you write on your own page carries the same three choices — just you, friends, or everyone — and it is shown to exactly that audience, in their feed and on your page. A post you write on a group page is visible to that group's members, and to nobody else; it stays visible to them if you later leave the group, unless you delete it.

Comments and likes are visible to whoever can see the post they are on. The person whose post it is, and a group's owner or editors, can delete comments left there — that is moderation, and it applies to comments you wrote.

Your page shows your name, photo, the profile details you filled in, your recent catches and your posts, each to the audience you gave it. An angler who has set their profile to not searchable also keeps their page closed to people outside their crew.

Groups you are invited to

Being invited to a group is not being in one. Until you accept, the group's members cannot see your page as a member, you are not counted in it, and none of your fishing data is shared with it. Declining is quiet — the owner is not told you said no, only that the invitation is no longer waiting.

When you accept you choose, per data type, what that group sees: spots, catches, waypoints. Those switches sit on top of each record's own visibility — a record still has to be marked as shared with the group before the switch matters — and you can change them at any time from the group's page. A group owner can also turn data sharing off for the whole group, and that wins over anything members ticked.

Tournaments

A tournament lives inside one group. The host sees who was invited and what each person answered — in, out, maybe, or nothing yet — along with any note you add. Other members see who is fishing it. We take no entry money and keep no results: what gets weighed is settled between you at the ramp.

Notifications

When someone comments on your post, likes it, adds you to a group or sends you a friend request, we store a row saying so — who did it, when, and a short preview of what they wrote. It is visible only to you. Notifications you have read are deleted automatically after two weeks.

Reporting content

When you report a post, comment, listing or angler, we store your report, your note, and a copy of what you reported, taken at that moment — the author can delete the original a second later, and then there would be nothing for us to look at. Admins can see who reported what; the person you reported is not told who reported them. Reporting is not the same as blocking, and doing one does not do the other.

Inviting someone

If you invite an angler by email, we store the address you typed, your note, and whether they joined, so the invite can be matched up when they arrive and so you can see what you sent. We email that address once. We do not add it to a mailing list, sell it, or email it again if they never sign up, and you can cancel an unaccepted invite from your crew page. Only invite people who would expect to hear from you.

Classifieds

A listing you post is visible to every signed-in angler — that is the point of a classifieds board, and there is no narrower setting for it. It shows what you wrote, your price, the location you typed, your name and your photo. Don't put your phone number or address in a listing: buyers reach you through the app's messages, and a listing you delete stops being visible, while a message you already sent does not.

Someone asking about your listing can message you even if your message setting is friends-only — posting an ad is an invitation to be contacted about it. Blocking still stops it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and log — until you delete them.
  • Sign-in links — 15 minutes, then swept.
  • Fetched agency reports — 120 days. Reports you posted are kept.
  • Error reports — 14 days.
  • Backups — up to 14 days, after which they are deleted.

Your choices

  • See and change everything about you in Settings → Profile.
  • Export your log and waypoints from Settings → Data & export, as JSON, CSV or GPX.
  • Delete your account by contacting us; your trips, spots, waypoints, messages and reports go with it. Messages you sent may remain in the other angler's copy of the conversation.
  • Stop email by not requesting sign-in links — we send no marketing email.

Security, honestly stated

Sign-in uses one-time links rather than passwords, sessions are stored as hashes, admin access needs a password plus an emailed code, and secrets in our configuration are encrypted at rest. Traffic is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and we won't claim otherwise; if we ever discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you.

Children

ElavoFishAI is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app rather than quietly editing this page.
