Rules of thumb

    Two things that will cost you real money. The LVS34 needs GLS 10 firmware 2.57 or newer — support for that transducer was added in that release, and older firmware simply will not run it. And disable all sonar before trailering: short-press the power key and turn off sonar transmission. The transducer is water-cooled, and sleep mode does not stop it pinging.

    Reading the barometer

    The trend matters far more than the number.

    TrendWhat's happeningWhat to do
    Falling fastFront inbound, usually 12–24 hrs outDrop everything and go. Fish feed hard ahead of weather. Cover water, fish shallower, be aggressive.
    Falling slowlyWeather buildingBetter than average. Normal patterns, more willing fish.
    SteadyStable air massNo edge either way. Fish the season and the light.
    Rising slowlyFront passed, clearingSlow down, downsize, fish tighter to cover.
    Rising fastBluebird post-frontal, high skyThe hardest bite there is. Small baits, slow, right on their nose. Takes a day or two to recover.
    Worth some skepticism. Whether fish sense pressure directly is genuinely debated — the research is thin and mixed. What's not debated is that pressure changes travel with the things that clearly do matter: cloud cover, wind, light and water temperature. Treat the barometer as a proxy for a weather change rather than a cause in itself.
